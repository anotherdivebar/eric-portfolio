  - "SQL analytics"
  - "BI dashboards"

  - "Defined shared lifecycle stages before moving data"
  - "Defined the fields and rules each system had to supply"
  - "Built repeatable reports for figures that leadership reviewed regularly"

  - "Reconciling definitions took time before the first reports could ship"
  - "Started with the pipeline and conversion measures the teams shared"
