---
title: "Lead routing automation"
summary: "I built a routing service that assigns each incoming request to an owner and tracks how long they have to respond."
why: "Requests waited for someone to assign them. With inconsistent ownership, the team could struggle to tell whether it had met a response deadline."
constraints:
  - "Sales teams needed to know who should respond and when to escalate"
  - "Incoming CRM records had missing or inconsistent fields"
  - "Compliance and leadership teams needed a history of routing decisions"
stack:
  - "C# / .NET services"
  - "JavaScript admin UI"
  - "PostgreSQL"
  - "CRM integrations and audit logs"
decisions:
  - "Used rules to identify the owner for each request"
  - "Added response timers, escalation paths, and a queue for exceptions"
  - "Made routing policies editable in the admin interface"
tradeoffs:
  - "Used rules so staff could explain and audit each assignment"
  - "Defining the initial rules took time, especially where ownership was unclear"
outcome: "Requests reached the right owner more consistently, and teams could respond faster."
order: 3
---
