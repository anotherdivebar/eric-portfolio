---
title: "Lead routing automation"
summary: "I built a routing service that assigns each incoming request to an owner and tracks how long they have to respond."
why: "Requests sat until someone assigned them, and the team could lose track of response deadlines."
constraints:
  - "Some incoming CRM records had missing or inconsistent fields."
  - "The compliance team and company leaders needed to see why a request went to a particular person."
stack:
  - "C# / .NET services"
  - "JavaScript admin UI"
  - "PostgreSQL"
  - "CRM integrations and audit logs"
decisions:
  - "I used explicit rules so staff could trace each assignment."
  - "I added rules for escalating requests when a response was overdue."
  - "I gave administrators a screen where they could change the rules themselves."
tradeoffs:
  - "Writing the first set of rules took time, especially when the team wasn’t sure who should respond."
outcome: "Incoming requests went to an owner with a response timer. Staff could review exceptions in a separate queue."
order: 3
---
