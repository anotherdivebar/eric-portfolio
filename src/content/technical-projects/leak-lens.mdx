## Overview

LeakLens is a revenue leak recovery product demo built to show how operators could find stalled quotes, dormant accounts, and follow-up gaps before they turn into lost revenue.

The work spans both sides of the funnel: a marketing experience that explains the problem clearly, and an authenticated product layer with connector management, leak triage, recovery workflows, and reporting.

## What it demonstrates

- **Connector and intake layer** for showing how revenue signals could enter one operating surface
- **Leak triage** so operators can review what is stale, what is blocked, and what needs action
- **Recovery workflows** with enough structure to feel usable, not like a concept slide
- **Reporting views** that connect triage work back to business outcomes
- **Product polish** through command-palette navigation, responsive layouts, runtime validation, and end-to-end smoke tests

## Why it matters

This project is less about another AI headline and more about product credibility. The goal was to make revenue recovery feel like an operator system: visible queues, reviewable actions, and a product surface that could survive a serious walkthrough.
