---
title: "Operations control center"
summary: "I built a dashboard where operations managers could check field capacity and job status and spot work that needed attention."
why: "Managers had to piece together several spreadsheets to find out where a job was at risk."
constraints:
  - "Field data arrived at different times and with uneven quality"
  - "Managers needed current figures that a small system could maintain"
  - "Minimal training time for operations teams"
stack:
  - "C# / .NET"
  - "PostgreSQL"
  - "JavaScript dashboard UI"
  - "Scheduled data sync"
decisions:
  - "Started with three to five measures the team used most often"
  - "Put jobs that needed intervention at the top of the dashboard"
  - "Added an export for leadership reports"
tradeoffs:
  - "Limited the number of metrics so teams could learn the dashboard quickly"
  - "Scheduled updates kept the system simple, with a delay between refreshes"
outcome: "Managers could see which jobs were at risk in one place and intervene sooner."
order: 5
---
