## From an estimate to a paid invoice

In the guided demo, a visitor takes an estimate through to a completed job and invoice. Recording a partial payment reduces the balance on that invoice. Changes stay in browser memory and reset on refresh.

In the estimating demo, visitors select costs from a local catalog, specify what the job includes or excludes, and choose which suggestions to accept from a rules-based assistant. When they save the proposal, it appears in the sample CRM and dispatch views.

The scheduling demo shows which tasks have to finish before others can start and where crew assignments overlap.

## Dispatch and maintenance demos by industry

A service contractor can start with dispatch. An equipment operator can start with maintenance. Examples for light manufacturing and restaurants show how purchasing, inventory, scheduling, production, and checks fit those businesses. The site also groups website, growth, and operations services under Digital Foundation, Growth Engine, and Operations System.

A visitor looking at maintenance arrives at the inquiry form with maintenance already selected.

## Subscription pricing and service costs

One subscription includes the supported core modules and accounts for ten full users. Buyers can add hands-on onboarding, custom implementation, or managed services. Links to retired packages still lead to the current inquiry form.

For each managed service, I listed the monthly work, what the customer handles, the setup cost, and the first-month total. I also listed usage charges from other providers, advertising costs, and any extra subscriptions a buyer may need. The team confirms early-access availability and whether it can take on the work.

## Walkthrough requests and email delivery

Someone requesting a walkthrough can suggest times and a time zone. A team member follows up to confirm availability.

<ProjectScreenshot
  src="/assets/atlas-beren-website/contact.png"

Before sending an inquiry through Resend, the Next.js route checks the request’s origin, content type, size, required fields, email address, and selected interests. A spam trap filters automated submissions. The form shows a confirmation only after Resend accepts the request. If Resend gives no clear answer, the form tells the visitor it couldn’t confirm delivery.

## Shared pricing and page design

The pricing, onboarding, and product pages all read prices and subscription terms from the same source. The site publishes canonical URLs, structured data, a sitemap, and robots directives.

The same paper, ink, and gold palette carries through the product pages, pricing, and inquiry forms on desktop and mobile.

## Evidence and current scope

As of October 2, 2026, automated checks covered demo steps, inquiry validation, topic selection, analytics filtering, and email-provider errors. A September 30 production build passed, along with nine checks covering the steps from finding a product to sending an inquiry. The review covered the pricing page on desktop and onboarding on mobile. It did not confirm a production deployment or measure how many visitors became paying customers.
