## What the demo covers

**Daily (operations)** — Executive overview of coverage velocity, exceptions, and AI recommendations; load board by status; carrier directory with trust and lanes; outreach workspace with threads and drafts; exception triage; document upload, extraction, and chase status.

**Grow** — Acquisition hub: prospect discovery (industry/size filters, suggested roles), pipeline stages with ARR hints, shipper expansion and whitespace.

**Run / platform** — Marketplace and data products surfaces; finance, factoring referrals, compliance checks before tender.

**Tools** — Analytics, exports and scheduled reports, capacity graphs, carrier-facing portal, AI assistant, playbooks.

**Cross-cutting** — Alerting, collaboration and presence, onboarding, settings and integrations.

**Who it is for** — Brokerages and 3PLs (coverage through compliance and finance); carrier sales (discovery, pipeline, outreach); carriers (portal for loads and status).

_Demo data — narrative simulation, not wired to a live TMS._
