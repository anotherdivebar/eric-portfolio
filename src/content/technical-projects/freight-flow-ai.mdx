## What it does

**Shipper Finder** - Surfaces likely shipper accounts across freight-heavy industries so a small team can work from better raw targets instead of generic lead lists.

**AI account brief + public contact finder** - Pulls together a quick account view, research angles, and public contact paths to cut down manual prep before outreach.

**Geographic workflow** - Lets reps work accounts by city and region so area-based calling and local clustering are built into the motion.

**Shared SRM pipeline** - Tracks accounts from discovery through active pipeline stages in a lighter-weight workflow than a traditional CRM.

## Why it is different

This is not a generic CRM. It is tailored to how freight sales teams actually prospect: finding shippers with strong freight signals, grouping nearby accounts for more efficient calling, surfacing public contact paths, and carrying qualified accounts into a shared relationship pipeline.

## Stack

Next.js, React, TypeScript, Supabase, server-side API routes, and AI-assisted enrichment and workflow tooling.
