## The sales workflow

FreightFlow AI organizes the steps between finding a potential shipper and deciding how to work the account. The workspace keeps research, geography, contact paths, and relationship stages close enough for a small team to use during daily prospecting.

### 1. Find likely shipper accounts

Shipper Finder surfaces companies across freight-heavy industries using public signals. The discovery view pairs a list of accounts with shipper details, fit scoring, and next-step context so a rep can review a candidate before carrying it into the sales workflow.

### 2. Prepare the account and contact path

An AI-assisted account brief brings together the company context, research angles, and possible approach for a call. Public contact research sits alongside that brief, with fallback search links and pages checked visible to the rep.

Contact coverage depends on the available public information. The workflow keeps those research paths available so the rep can continue checking an account when enrichment is incomplete.

### 3. Work a city or region

Geography is part of the sales workflow. Reps can group nearby accounts and work by city or region, using the account brief and nearby prospects to prepare a focused calling session.

### 4. Carry the account into a shared pipeline

The shipper pipeline tracks relationship stages from discovery through qualified, contacted, proposal, and awarded. Research becomes part of a shared account-development process, giving the team visibility into where each shipper stands.

## Architecture and scope

The workspace uses Next.js, React, and TypeScript, with Supabase for the shared data layer and server-side API routes supporting discovery and AI-assisted enrichment. The product scope centers on shipper prospecting and relationship management: finding an account, researching it, and keeping the next stage visible to the team.

The main design decision was to connect those tasks in one flow. Public data coverage and enrichment quality remain practical constraints, so the interface includes contact research paths and enough account context for a rep to review the next step.
