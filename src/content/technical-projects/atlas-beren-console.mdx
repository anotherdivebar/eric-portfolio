## Connected records and handoffs

The console links original records by ID. When a deal closes, the team can keep using its existing project. An invoice imported from accounting can link back to the original, but a matching name or amount alone isn’t enough to join the two.

If the link is missing or two records disagree, someone has to check them. The console saves a required link and its record in one transaction. If someone else has already edited the record, the revision check stops an older edit from overwriting it.

## Revenue decisions with evidence

Once a rep submits a forecast, that version stays on file. A manager can look back and see which deals actually closed. Scheduled reports also preserve their filters, dates, and underlying figures.

Before a rep promises a start date, they can compare up to three open deals with draft estimates. The comparison shows which combinations the crew can cover, when materials would run short, and what a larger deposit would do for cash. The rep can carry the chosen terms into the draft quote for review.

## Connected profit and costs

The same invoice appears in customer, project, campaign, and financial reports. It contributes revenue once. The calculation accounts separately for tax, payments, deposits, and linked accounting copies.

You can trace time, materials, other job costs, returns, credits, and reversals back to their source. When a supplier bill and its payment both come in from accounting, someone has to identify which entry records the cost so it only counts once. If the source changes, it may need another look.

The job history puts approved labor, invoices, receipts, credits, and deposits in date order. An owner can trace a low margin to a specific cost, then see how much the customer still owes. Costs that still need assigning stay visible.

## Workspace AI Helper

Someone using **Ask AI** might ask “Which invoices are overdue?” or “What is blocking this project?” The assistant can search the records that person has permission to read and link to the sources behind its answer. It works across all seven modules.

I added decision reviews for individual records. A sales rep can compare a buyer’s email with the exclusions in a quote, check the passages behind a finding, correct it, and record what the buyer decided. The same review can use a selected PDF page, a photo, or a text excerpt.

For supported records, the reviewer can turn a decision into a proposed edit in Workspace Updates. Approving it runs the same permission, validation, and revision checks as editing the record directly.

## Field video and project manager calls

A technician can record a narrated walkthrough on an assigned job. If the connection drops, the device keeps the clip queued for upload. The office can read an equipment label on the device or use a configured video model to suggest findings. Each finding has a timestamp so a reviewer can replay the moment and correct the observation before sharing it.

During a live call, a project manager can tap a valve on the technician’s video. The technician sees a ring follow that feature as the camera moves. If the camera loses sight of the valve, the ring disappears. The manager can publish a marked still with the decision, owner, and next action. The technician assigned to the job can open it later.

With **Room Memory**, the manager can use that still or a new photo to leave a voice note on a specific piece of equipment. On a return visit, the technician’s phone matches the saved view to the camera image. They can play the note and slide between visits to inspect what changed. The phone processes the live comparison frames locally.

## Automation that explains its actions

The scheduled worker checks for ready tasks every five minutes. Before executing an action it rechecks the operator’s permissions and whether that action already ran.

Run history shows each attempt, failure, and completed step. An administrator can cancel eligible work with a reason or retest a failure against the latest records. If an outside service leaves it unclear whether an action went through, the console holds the retry until someone checks.

## Customer portal

Customers can open the saved version of a quote, approve it, request service, choose an available appointment, and see their service history and bills. Access requires a verified customer identity. Internal notes and private costs stay with the office.

A customer planning equipment replacements can move assets between budget years, check the service and warranty history, and approve the first phase from its saved quotes. They can also ask about an exclusion or line item and open the part of their shared quote that answers it.

## Business Simulator

An owner can compare up to eight proposed jobs over 13 weeks. The simulator calculates cash in US dollars. Moving a start date can reveal a crew or stock conflict. Changing a deposit shows its effect on daily cash.

A saved prediction keeps the source data and assumptions used for that run. An estimator can replay it later and compare the forecast with reviewed costs and hours from the job. The estimator chooses when to apply terms to the draft quote, and dispatch still books the visit.

## Migration and recovery

CSV and spreadsheet imports carry over customers, equipment, service history, price lists, agreements, documents, and opening balances. The preview shows which rows need attention and why they’re on hold. Once approved, the import saves in one transaction. Submitting it again returns the original result.

The recovery tools package database records and documents in an encrypted backup with a manifest listing its contents. Tests included restoring synthetic records in isolation, checking permissions, and handling simultaneous requests. Recovering a customer’s live system also depends on how its hosting, storage, identity provider, and other services are set up.

## Delivery and boundaries

The public AI Studio uses fictional jobs, buyers, and equipment records. In a customer workspace, access to clips, quotes, and reviewed decisions follows the person’s role and job assignment. Field calls between networks require a configured relay. Video analysis uses the workspace’s model connection and budget.
