## The business problem

Winning a customer is only the beginning. The research behind the opportunity, the promises made in a conversation, and the agreed scope all need to survive the move into delivery. Completed work then needs an accurate invoice and a clear view of its costs.

I built ABS Console around that customer journey. My work spans product design, the interface, business logic, database permissions, integrations, and verification. The aim is practical: give each person the context to make the next decision and keep the commercial consequences visible.

## Connected records and handoffs

A shared customer identity connects prospect research, CRM opportunities, estimates, projects, field jobs, and financial records. Each module keeps its original records; explicit relationships make those records available wherever the work continues.

Starting an estimate from a project carries its customer and project context forward. Accepting that estimate creates a linked job. A won opportunity can reuse its delivery project, while an imported accounting copy can link back to the original invoice. Matching names or amounts alone never establish identity.

Established connections follow the workflow automatically. Missing or conflicting relationships prompt a focused review. Required connections and their source changes save together, while revision checks prevent an older editor from overwriting newer work.

## Revenue decisions with evidence

The sales workspace connects relationship management to inspection: follow-up queues, configurable stages, scoped reports, custom fields, arithmetic formulas, and saved dashboards. Each report can open the deals behind its totals.

Quotas and manager commitments sit alongside immutable forecast submissions. Teams can compare an earlier submission with subsequently recorded wins without rewriting the original forecast. Personal scheduled reports preserve their filters, resolved dates, and source snapshot for later review.

These choices make sales judgment easier to examine. Opportunity value stays distinct from invoiced revenue; missing close dates and incomplete values remain visible.

## Connected profit and costs

Financial, project, customer, and campaign views use a shared source model for invoiced revenue and recorded costs. Issued invoices contribute revenue once. Tax, payments, deposits, and explicitly linked accounting copies do not become additional revenue.

Approved time, actual materials, additional project costs, inventory returns, credits, and reversals retain their original identities. Imported expenses require classification so a supplier bill and its payment cannot silently count as two costs. Changed evidence can invalidate an earlier review.

Reports keep currencies separate and identify incomplete costs or ambiguous attribution. The resulting view helps an owner understand contribution and cash exposure while preserving the path back to the work behind each number.

## Workspace AI Helper

**Ask AI** is available across all seven modules. A user can ask which follow-ups need attention, what is blocking a project, or which invoices are overdue. The answer reads accessible records and links to the sources it actually retrieved.

The same entry point can turn a conversation into prepared updates. A note about a customer payment and requested service date can produce separate proposed steps for review. Missing amounts, ambiguous customers, and required receipt or acceptance evidence remain explicit.

I separated interpretation from execution. Read tools answer questions; reviewed commands use the existing module permissions, validators, and revision checks. Specialized work such as drawing analysis or accounting review opens the appropriate workspace. Private conversation history and metered provider usage follow the user's current access.

## Automation that explains its actions

Native recipes expose their conditions, matching values, selected branch, and proposed effects before execution. Scheduled work uses a database worker with bounded batches, current-permission checks, and replay protection.

Run history preserves individual attempts, failures, and completed effects. Administrators can cancel eligible work with a reason or retest a failed run against current evidence. An uncertain external response is held for reconciliation instead of triggering a blind repeat.

This makes automation a process the operator can inspect and recover, with a clear connection between the source event and the resulting action.

## Customer portal

The customer portal carries the relationship beyond the sale. Customers can review an exact saved quote, approve it, request service, select reviewed appointment options, and inspect customer-facing service history and billing.

Access is tied to the intended verified customer identity. Internal notes and private costs remain outside that view. Changed quotes require a fresh review; appointment booking rechecks current staffing and reservations before confirming the same underlying job.

## Business Simulator

The simulator tests proposed jobs against a 13-week view of qualified labor, material availability, and daily cash. It can compare deposit requirements, later start dates, or additional staffing using the same underlying assumptions.

A saved prediction freezes the inputs, source timestamps, and calculated results. Later observations retain evidence so the team can compare the plan with recorded actuals. Running a scenario does not book a job, reserve a crew, purchase stock, or change accounting.

It reflects how I approach sustainable growth: understand the delivery and cash constraints before treating another sale as an automatic win.

## Migration and recovery

A useful business system also needs a credible way to adopt it. Reviewed CSV and spreadsheet imports preserve source identities for customers, equipment, service history, price libraries, agreements, documents, and opening balances. Previews expose held rows and conflicts before an atomic commit; repeat submissions recover the same result.

Recovery tooling creates encrypted database and document bundles with verification manifests. The verification approach includes an actual isolated restore of synthetic records, permission checks, and concurrent-request tests. That exercises the recovery path while keeping a production recovery plan dependent on the real hosting, identity, storage, and provider configuration.

## Delivery and boundaries

The application combines tenant-scoped database policies with module permissions, guarded commands, and explicit review at consequential handoffs. Unit and database behavior tests check calculations and access; browser fixtures exercise complete workflows with fictional records.

This case study reflects the source reviewed on **October 28, 2026**, at revision **b048d86**. The console includes email, calendar, accounting, payment, research, and automation adapters. Each provider still needs appropriate configuration, authorization, and live acceptance checks before its workflow is enabled for a customer. Marketing publishing and live ad-platform management remain outside the current supported workflow.

The product demonstrates how I connect customer discovery, relationship-based sales, operational delivery, and financial discipline in one system.
