## Situation
Multiple teams were shipping in parallel without a shared delivery model. Dependencies were hard to see, risks surfaced late, and accountability was unclear.

## Action
Built a program framework with milestones, dependency tracking, weekly risk reviews, and executive updates.

## Result
Delivery timelines became easier to trust, risks surfaced earlier, and stakeholders had a clearer path for decisions and escalation.
