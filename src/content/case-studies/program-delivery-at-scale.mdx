## Context

At Vale Field Services, I worked across the commercial and delivery sides of multi-site programs for national restaurant, retail, and commercial customers. Execution depended on coordinating distributed teams and vendors while balancing schedule, quality, cost, and the customer experience.

The operating challenge extended beyond scheduling a technician. Each rollout needed a workable plan before deployment, clear ownership during execution, and a reliable path through issue resolution, documentation, and invoicing readiness.

## Before deployment

I served as a strategic pre-deployment partner, identifying feasibility constraints, site-readiness gaps, installation risks, and support requirements. That work informed the execution model and surfaced dependencies before teams arrived on site.

## During delivery

I owned operating frameworks spanning customer onboarding, project planning, documentation, dispatch, subcontractor management, and field execution. SOPs, training materials, performance standards, and escalation paths gave distributed teams a consistent way to work and raise issues.

The program structure connected those individual activities: who was responsible, what needed to be ready, and how an issue moved toward a decision.

## Through closeout

Delivery continued through issue resolution, invoicing readiness, and post-work quality follow-through. Including those steps in the operating model kept the scope broader than the installation date and connected field completion with customer and business requirements.

## Business context

During my July 2019–October 2024 tenure, I helped scale the company from startup stage to approximately $4M in annual revenue. My contribution was the processes, vendor networks, training, QA, and delivery infrastructure needed to support repeatable national growth.

That operating experience also informs the [internal systems I build](/technical-projects): clear ownership, visible exceptions, and workflows that carry work through completion.
