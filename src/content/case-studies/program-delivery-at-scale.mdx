## From employee one to leading operations

I was Vale’s first employee, installing equipment and getting to know customers on site. As we hired, I moved into the office to plan work for the crews. I eventually took responsibility for business operations outside finance.

## <span id="building-the-team-and-operating-structure">Four field supervisors and a vendor network</span>

I managed four field supervisors. The company also had 29 field technicians and administrative staff. I built our vendor network, wrote procedures, and set the training and quality standards our crews used.

## Restaurant technology across the U.S. and Canada

We installed point-of-sale systems and other restaurant technology at Taco Bell, Dairy Queen, Scooter’s, McDonald’s, and KFC locations. A typical rollout covered hundreds of stores. We also handled technology and facilities work for retail and commercial customers.

## Before deployment

Before dispatch, I checked which restaurants were ready for installation with the customer. That determined which stores I could assign to a crew and which needed more work before a technician made the trip.

## During delivery

I coordinated dispatch with our four supervisors and subcontractors. If an installer couldn’t resolve a problem at a store, the crew had a supervisor to call.

## Through closeout

I checked the finished installation against our standards and followed up on any open issues. The office used the crew’s field report to prepare the invoice.
