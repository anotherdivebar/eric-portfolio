## From employee one to leading operations

I was Vale’s first employee, installing equipment and getting to know customers on site. As we hired, I moved into the office to plan work for the crews. I eventually took responsibility for business operations outside finance.

## Building the team and operating structure

I managed four field supervisors. The company also had 29 field technicians and administrative staff. I built our vendor network, wrote procedures, and set the training and quality standards our crews used.

## Restaurant technology across the U.S. and Canada

We installed point-of-sale systems and other restaurant technology at Taco Bell, Dairy Queen, Scooter’s, McDonald’s, and KFC locations. A typical rollout covered hundreds of stores. Our other customers included retail and commercial businesses with technology and facilities programs.

## Before deployment

I worked with the customer on the plan, checked which locations were ready, and identified installation risks and support needs. That helped me decide which crews and subcontractors to send and what had to happen before they arrived.

## During delivery

I coordinated dispatch with supervisors and subcontractors. Crews knew whom to contact for problems they couldn’t resolve on site.

## Through closeout

I followed up on outstanding issues, checked the finished work against our quality standards, and made sure each job was ready to invoice. Field reports had to capture the details the person preparing the invoice would need. [Explore my systems work](/technical-projects)
