## From employee one to leading operations

When I joined Vale Field Services in July 2019, I worked on site and got to know customers during installations. As we hired, I moved into the office to plan work for the crews. I eventually took responsibility for business operations outside finance.

## Building the team and operating structure

By the time the company had 35 employees, I managed four field supervisors. The company also had 29 field technicians and administrative staff. I built our vendor network, wrote procedures, and set the training and quality standards our crews used.

## Restaurant technology across the U.S. and Canada

We installed point-of-sale systems and other restaurant technology at Taco Bell, Dairy Queen, Scooter’s, McDonald’s, and KFC locations. We worked across the U.S. and Canada, and a typical rollout covered hundreds of stores. Our other customers included retail and commercial businesses with technology and facilities programs.

## Before deployment

I worked with the customer on the plan, checked which locations were ready, and identified installation risks and support needs. That helped me decide which crews and subcontractors to send and what had to happen before they arrived.

## During delivery

I coordinated dispatch and installation with our four supervisors and vendor network. Crews had written procedures, training materials, and a contact for problems they couldn’t resolve on site.

## Through closeout

I followed up on outstanding issues, checked the finished work against our quality standards, and made sure each job was ready to invoice.

## Business context

By the end of my tenure in October 2024, Vale had reached about $4M in annual revenue. My contribution was getting the people, vendors, and procedures in place to deliver the work we won.
