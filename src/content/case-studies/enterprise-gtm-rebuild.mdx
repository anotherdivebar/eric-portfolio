## <span id="define-the-lifecycle-before-the-dashboard">From website inquiry to account history</span>

<p id="where-this-approach-applies">If a customer called back, their last conversation might be in someone else’s inbox or spreadsheet. I built a CRM that kept those conversations with the account, sent website inquiries to the sales team, and synced activity with Acumatica.</p>

## <span id="make-handoffs-reviewable">Putting a number on open bids</span>

Opportunity Radar kept the list of open bids up to date. For the forecast itself, I built a separate tool called BD Forecast in Google Sheets. Apps Script applied the same weights to each team’s bids based on how far along they were, then saved a copy of the 90-day forecast.

## <span id="build-a-recurring-decision-loop">Reviewing the pipeline with leadership</span>

When I sat down with leadership, we looked beyond the next 90 days. I tracked what we might win over two years, what we’d already put in front of customers, and what we’d actually closed. Each had its own total.
