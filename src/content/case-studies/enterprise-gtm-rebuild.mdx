## <span id="define-the-lifecycle-before-the-dashboard">From website inquiry to account history</span>

<p id="where-this-approach-applies">Sales conversations lived in inboxes and spreadsheets. I built a CRM that routed website inquiries to the sales team and synced account activity with Acumatica. Before calling a customer back, a rep could pull up the previous conversation.</p>

## <span id="make-handoffs-reviewable">Putting a number on open bids</span>

Opportunity Radar refreshed the list of open opportunities on a schedule. In a separate tool, BD Forecast, I used Google Sheets and Apps Script to apply the same stage weights across teams and save a snapshot of the 90-day forecast.

## <span id="build-a-recurring-decision-loop">Reviewing the pipeline with leadership</span>

For longer-term planning, I tracked opportunities over 24 months. In leadership reviews, I kept the value of that pipeline separate from the value of proposals we had presented and work we had closed.
