## Situation
Growth outpaced the operating model. Data definitions were inconsistent, handoffs were slow, and leaders did not have a clean view of where revenue was moving or getting stuck.

## Action
Unified lifecycle definitions, rebuilt routing logic, and implemented a weekly operating cadence that gave GTM teams one shared view of pipeline movement and follow-up risk.

## Result
Forecasting inputs became more consistent, conversion tracking was standardized across teams, and ownership was clearer across the lead-to-cash path.
