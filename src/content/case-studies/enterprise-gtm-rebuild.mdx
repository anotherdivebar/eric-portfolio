## Define the lifecycle before the dashboard

<p id="where-this-approach-applies">I follow one request from the sales inbox through delivery and billing. At each step, I ask who takes over and what they need from the previous team.</p>

I work with sales, marketing, finance, and customer success to agree on the evidence needed to move a deal forward. Each record shows its owner, status, next action, and anything it’s waiting on.

## Make handoffs reviewable

Each new request goes to a named person with a deadline for a response. If the rules can’t assign it or follow-up is overdue, it goes into a queue the team reviews.

## Build a recurring decision loop

In a weekly sales review I look for deals that have changed stage, stalled, or lost an owner. We use the same definitions the team uses during the week and decide who will follow up on each exception.
