## Situation
Manual validation and delayed risk signals slowed response time. Churn risk was visible too late, and teams were spending too much time reconciling data before acting.

## Action
Automated enrichment, validation, and alerting across CRM and lifecycle tools so teams could respond from a shared set of signals.

## Result
Handoffs were standardized, SLA adherence was easier to monitor, and retention risks became easier to catch before they escalated.
