## Start with the input

<p id="where-this-approach-applies">Sales and support need to agree on what the fields mean. If a customer record is incomplete, I send it for review instead of guessing who should receive it.</p>

## Connect the signal to an action

For a pipeline change or upcoming renewal, the alert should say what changed, who should follow up, and when a response is due. If the deadline passes, the workflow shows the overdue item and who can help resolve it.

## Review the exceptions

When the team reviews missed handoffs, we distinguish a one-off data error from a rule that keeps sending valid records to the wrong person.
