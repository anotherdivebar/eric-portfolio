## From an existing relationship to a Walmart project

<p id="developing-relationships-across-industrial-and-commercial-markets">Walmart needed emergency lighting and relay work at locations in the Midwest and Southeast. A relationship I’d built at Vale Field Services helped me win the work for Kansas Electric. After the sale I remained the customer’s point of contact and oversaw the project managers responsible for both regions.</p>

## Building a dedicated sales function

When I arrived, project managers handled sales alongside the jobs they were delivering. I took responsibility for developing accounts with industrial and commercial contractors and business owners. I set up a sales process, recorded customer conversations, and reviewed the pipeline and forecast with leadership.

## Taking ownership of marketing

I also became Director of Marketing. I led the rebrand, rebuilt the website, designed signage and billboards, and ran digital campaigns with about $5,000 in monthly ad spend. I also helped with recruiting.

## Constraints

<p id="building-the-systems-behind-the-function">We had a small team and little engineering time. Crews worked changing schedules and sometimes lacked a connection on site. New tools had to fit around active jobs and the spreadsheets people already relied on.</p>

## Architecture choices

**Offline field reports.** I used service worker caching and IndexedDB to keep reports on the device until the crew had a connection. Apps Script stored the media in Drive and the report rows in Sheets.

<p id="what-i-owned"><strong>Rollout and maintenance.</strong> I built the public site with Astro and Tailwind, documented the procedures, and maintained the internal apps, their supporting services, and the GitHub workflows used to ship changes.</p>
