## <span id="start-with-the-handoff">Closing out a restaurant installation</span>

At Vale, I sent crews to install point-of-sale systems in hundreds of restaurants. I could look at the schedule and see who I’d sent to a store. What I couldn’t tell from the schedule was whether we were ready to bill. For that I needed the crew’s report. I’d check what they’d finished and follow up on anything they still had to fix before the office sent the invoice.

## <span id="define-the-signal-before-building-the-report">Recording the job before leaving the site</span>

At Kansas Electric, a technician might finish a job somewhere with no signal. I built an app so they could record a video right there. Prompts on the screen reminded them what to cover, and the recording stayed on the device until it could sync.

<p id="give-exceptions-somewhere-to-go">The app filled report fields from the transcript. The technician checked the summary before submitting it. The office got the report in Google Sheets, and the video went to Drive.</p>
