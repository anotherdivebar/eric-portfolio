## <span id="start-with-the-handoff">Closing out a restaurant installation</span>

At Vale, I coordinated point-of-sale installations across hundreds of restaurants. Before I could pass a store to the office for invoicing, I had to check the finished work and follow up on anything the crew still needed to resolve. A visit on the dispatch schedule told me a crew had been assigned. I needed the field report to know what happened at the store.

## <span id="define-the-signal-before-building-the-report">Recording the job before leaving the site</span>

At Kansas Electric, a technician could finish the work somewhere with no connection. I built a field reporting app that kept the recording on the device until it could sync. Prompts on the video screen guided the technician through the report while the job was still in front of them.

<p id="give-exceptions-somewhere-to-go">The app filled report fields from the transcript. The technician checked the summary before submitting it. The office got the report in Google Sheets, and the video went to Drive.</p>
